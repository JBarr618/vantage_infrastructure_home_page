Topic 1: Introduction to Railroad Signal and Train Control

Welcome to the fascinating world of railroad signals and train control! This course will guide you through the fundamental principles and technologies that ensure the safe and efficient movement of trains. Understanding signal systems is crucial for anyone involved in the railroad signal industry, from engineers and conductors to maintenance personnel and safety officers.

The story of railroads in America began in the early 19th century. The Baltimore and Ohio Railroad, also known as the B&O Railroad, is generally considered the first common carrier railroad in the United States. Its construction began on July 4, 1828, and it officially opened its first section in 1830.

Trains came before the widespread adoption of cars in America. For nearly a century, railroads were the dominant mode of long-distance travel and freight transport across the United States.
